# Quebec compliance and copy rules

## Credentials (hard ban)
Never mention or imply these anywhere (copy, code, metadata, alt text, JSON-LD, comments):
CISSP, CIPP (any variant), CPA as a credential of CyberSentinelPro, Barreau du Québec, "legal counsel", "avocat", "lawyer".
Accounting firms as a partner audience are written "cabinets comptables / accounting firms".
Privacy counsel may be named only as a partner audience, never as something CyberSentinelPro is or provides.
Authority is positioned on enterprise security architecture, hands-on AI adversarial engineering, OWASP LLM / Agentic standards, and live tooling.

## Legal disclaimer
Footer and the audit tier carry: "CyberSentinelPro fournit des services d'ingénierie en cybersécurité et en conformité opérationnelle, et non des avis juridiques." and its English twin.

## Law 25
- Consent is opt-in, granular, and as easy to refuse as to accept. No pre-checked boxes.
- Lead forms state the purpose of collection next to the submit button and link the privacy page.
- `consent_contact` is required; `consent_marketing` is separate and optional.
- No analytics, pixels or third-party embeds before consent. Cal.com loads on click only.
- The privacy page names the privacy officer contact, processors, hosting locations, retention and rights.
- Law 25 statutory figures used in copy: administrative monetary penalties up to $10M or 2 % of worldwide turnover; penal fines up to $25M or 4 %. Do not invent other figures.

## Language (Bill 96)
- French is the default locale and is never less prominent than English.
- Write Quebec business French: courriel (not e-mail), témoin (not cookie in prose), rendez-vous, ÉFVP, renseignements personnels, entreprise.
- Use French typography: non-breaking space before `:` `;` `?` `!` and inside « », `$` after the amount (`5 000 $`).
- English amounts: `$5,000 CAD`. Dates displayed `DD/MM/YYYY`, stored ISO 8601.
- Phone format `(514) 555-0123`; postal code `H7X 1A1` style.
